About

About Us

Olla M&P Solution is an international patient agency registered with Daegu Metropolitan City under Korea's Act on Support for Overseas Expansion of Healthcare and Attraction of International Patients. We help patients from abroad access Korea's world-class medical care with confidence. Our office is in Mir Medi Tower, in the medical district of Suseong-gu, Daegu.

For patients, we remove the language and cultural barriers. For hospitals, we provide a trusted, compliant channel for international patients.

  • Registered international patient agency (Reg. No. A-2026-03-02-06726, valid 2026-03-04 to 2029-03-03)
  • In-house medical interpreters and coordinators
  • Partner hospitals vetted for accreditation and international patient experience
  • Strict protection of your personal and medical data
