About us

Olla Guide is a guide service for international patients run by Olla M&P Solution. Olla M&P Solution is a foreign patient facilitator registered with Daegu Metropolitan City under the Korean Act on Support for Overseas Expansion of Medical Services and Attraction of Foreign Patients.

We help international patients get treatment in Korea with peace of mind, through hospital matching, medical interpreting and help with preparing your stay. For hospitals, we aim to be a reliable point of contact for international patients.

Services

We help with consultation, hospital matching and trip preparation, and explain the services the hospital provides.

Consultation for international patients

We first listen to your symptoms, the care you want and your travel dates, online or by messenger.

Hospital and doctor matching

We suggest partner hospitals that fit your condition, budget and length of stay, and help you book appointments.

Medical interpreting

We connect you with a medical interpreter for your language. During consultations, surgery and hospital stays, the hospital’s international patient coordinator is with you.

Visa and stay preparation

We help with documents for the medical tourism visa (C-3-3, G-1-10) and explain how to get from the airport to the hospital.

Estimates, payment and insurance

We tell you the expected cost and payment options before treatment. We guide you on getting overseas insurance claim documents from the hospital.

Follow-up after you return

After you go home, the hospital checks on your recovery and offers remote consultations. We help you stay connected.

How it works

  1. 01

    Ask for a consultation

    Tell us your symptoms and the care you want, online or by messenger.

  2. 02

    Hospital matching and estimate

    We suggest hospitals, an expected cost and a schedule.

  3. 03

    Preparing your trip

    We explain how to prepare visa documents, flights and accommodation.

  4. 04

    Treatment with an interpreter

    You see the doctor with the hospital’s international patient coordinator. We also connect you with a medical interpreter.

  5. 05

    Follow-up

    After you go home, the hospital checks on your recovery and offers remote consultations. We help you stay connected.
