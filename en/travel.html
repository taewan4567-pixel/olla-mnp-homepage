Sightseeing Guide

Places worth visiting by region, for patients and companions during their stay. Please plan around your treatment schedule and take it easy.

This is travel information and is separate from our medical services.

Choosing where to stay

The Olla M&P Solution office is in Mir Medi Tower, Suseong-gu, Daegu. The nearest metro station is Suseong Market (Line 3). Check where your hospital is before choosing a room.

Close to the hospital

If you visit the hospital every day or have early appointments, choose a place within walking distance or a short taxi ride.

A building with an elevator

Stairs can be hard after surgery or a procedure. Check that there is an elevator in advance.

A quiet room

While recovering, a quiet room away from main roads and nightlife areas makes it easier to rest.

Travelling with a companion

Consider a twin room or a place with a small kitchen.

Emergency numbers

If you have an emergency while in Korea, call 119 first.

  • 119Ambulance and fire (24 hours)
  • 1339KDCA call center (infectious disease, quarantine)
  • 1330Korea Travel Hotline (24 hours, from abroad +82-2-1330)
  • 1345Immigration Contact Center (20 languages)
